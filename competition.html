Competition terms

  • One entry per person. Entrants must be 18 or over.
  • The requested date is a preference and is subject to availability; the winner's date will be confirmed with Private Disco.
  • The winner is chosen by the Private Disco team based on the entry, and will be contacted by WhatsApp or email.
  • The prize has no cash alternative, is non-transferable, and excludes venue hire, food and drink.
  • Private Disco may share the winning celebration (with permission) on Instagram.
